Mesh Assistant
Design system, draft

A phone app for when the network is down. It reaches a hub through other phones.

One conversation. It tells you what's happening, then lets you ask.

White, black, and three colours that each mean one thing.

Know what's happening.

Fictional scenario: a power cut in south London, mobile networks down.

    Three colours. One meaning each.

    Colour always comes with a shape and a word, so it works for colour-blind people too. Yellow is only ever a fill, never text.

    The cards.

    The hub picks one of these and fills in the words. The phone draws it. The model never invents a layout.

    Rules.

    The short version of PRINCIPLES.md.

    Most urgent firstOne idea per message, one action per card.
    Say what isn't knownNever guess. Promise to follow up, then do.
    Show the sourceWho said it and when, under every message. Official words are never reworded.
    Hint, then revealShow that information exists. Open it only when asked.
    44-point targetsStressed hands miss small buttons. Text scales with the phone's setting.
    Light on batteryNo model on the phone, no looping animation, the map drawn only when opened.
