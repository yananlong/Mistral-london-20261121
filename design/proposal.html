“What can I find?” lives under the topic icons — and every answer opens as a pop-up on the same screen

Below the six square topic icons sits a second grid of things you can find nearby: water points, food banks, doctors, defibrillators, A&E, charging, fuel, toilets, transport, alerts, people. They are grouped so the list stays short: tap a group to see its categories, tap a category to see places, tap a place for the detail. Nothing opens a new screen — it is always a sheet sliding up over the same screen, with a back arrow.

The left menu also loads the other proposals from the gap list: news, SOS, safe-and-well, camera orientation, voice ask + read-aloud, and the first-aid guide. Pick one, then click inside the phone.
